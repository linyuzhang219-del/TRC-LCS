# Code of Conduct

We want TRC-LCS to be a constructive, technically focused open-source community.

Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, use a private repository contact channel rather than a public issue whenever possible.
